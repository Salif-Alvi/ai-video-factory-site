AI Video Factory

AI Video Factory is a small private tool used by one person to prepare short videos and publish them to that person's own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts.

It uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's own videos, to read statistics for the owner's own channels, and to read comments on the owner's own videos. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign in to it.

Channels that use it: Doctor Riddleton, Load Bearing.
